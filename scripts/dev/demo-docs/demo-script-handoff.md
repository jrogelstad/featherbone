## The ask

A click-by-click demo script for the life of one order in SupplyChain, in sections by functional area, and a narrated video that performs it. Sections: Dashboard, Sales, Purchasing, Production, Execution, Shipping, Accounting. Storyline: check the dashboard in the morning and drill into a couple of data points; a new sales order comes in (enter it, check the promise date, confirm); purchasing buys components and the shortages; production releases the work order and prints the traveler; the floor reports operation completions in Execution; shipping ships; accounting invoices and collects. The flow follows SupplyChain's help article `common/help/order-to-cash.md` (make-to-order example).
