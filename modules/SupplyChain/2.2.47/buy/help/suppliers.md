# Suppliers and settings

## Suppliers

Suppliers are business entities to which you can place a purchase order for products or services.

- **Number** — A manually created unique identifier.
- **Name** — The name of the supplier.
- **Address** — The mailing address of the supplier.
- **Contact** — A primary contact copied to purchase orders for the supplier.
- **Is Active** — Indicates the supplier is being actively used. Only active suppliers appear on the list of results when searching suppliers form a purchase order.
- **Currency** — Default currency for purchase orders with the supplier.
- **Terms** — The default financial payment terms that copy to new purchase orders with the supplier.
- **Tax Exempt** — Indicates whether you are filed as tax exempt with the supplier. Information only.
- **Account Number** — Your account number with the supplier
- **Ship Method** — The default shipping method that copies to new purchase orders with the supplier.
- **Incoterms** — The default legal arrangement of responsibility when ordered goods are shipped. This value copies to new purchase orders with the supplier.
- **Score** — A ranking between 0 and 100 indicating the quality of the supplier.
- **Contacts** — A list of alternate contacts for the supplier. If Send PO is checked, the email of the contact will be included on the “to” addresses when sending a purchase order.

## Supplier contacts

This is simply a searchable reference list of all contacts associated with a supplier.

## Item suppliers

A searchable list of all products and services associated with a supplier. To edit an item supplier you must open the item it belongs to.

## Buy settings

- **Purchase Order** — The layout to use when creating a purchase order PDF.
- **On Release** — An action to automatically run when a purchase order is released. The options are:
- **Do nothing** — Don’t take any additional action.
- **Email** — Send an email with the purchase order PDF attached.
- **Download** — Save the purchase order PDF to your downloads folder.
- **Open PDF** — Open the purchase order PDF in a new browser tab.
- **Print** — Print the purchase order PDF.
- **Packing Slip** — The layout to use when printing outside process shipment packing slips.
- **Print On Ship** — When set to true, a packing slip automatically prints when an outside process shipment is shipped.
- **Mail Text** — The default text to appear in the body of email when you send a purchase order.
